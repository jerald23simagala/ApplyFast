Usage Guide

Getting Started

  1. Install ApplyFast from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Navigate to NetSuite's Accept Customer Payment screen
  3. The ApplyFast interface will automatically appear on the right side of the screen
  4. Click the header to expand the interface if it's minimized

Basic Usage

Paste invoice references (one per line) into the text area. Each reference will be matched and applied for its remaining open balance.

Example:
INV12345
PO67890
REF123

Partial Payments

To apply a partial payment, use the format: INV12345=100.00 where the amount is in US format (comma for thousands, dot for decimal).

Example:
INV12345=439.26
PO67890=1,234.56
REF123=100.00

Excel Integration

You can copy and paste data directly from Excel - the extension automatically detects the format:

  • Two columns: Invoice Reference | Payment Amount - automatically formats to INV123=100.00
  • Three columns: Invoice Reference | Discount Amount | Payment Amount - automatically formats to INV123|10.00|100.00 and populates both discount and payment fields
Excel Format (Two Columns):
INV12345    100.00
PO67890    439.26

Excel Format (Three Columns with Discount):
INV12345    10.00    100.00
PO67890    5.00    439.26

When you paste 3-column data, ApplyFast automatically fills both the "Disc. Taken" and payment amount fields in NetSuite.

Best Practices

  • Prepare your reference and amount data in a spreadsheet before pasting
  • Use consistent invoice reference formats
  • Verify matched invoices before saving the payment
  • For large batches (500+ lines), the extension can process them in under 2 minutes

Matching Process

  1. Paste your invoice list into the text area
  2. Click the "✅ Match Lines" button
  3. The extension will automatically:
    • Find matching invoice references in the payment sublist
    • Check the checkbox for matched lines
    • Fill in the payment amounts (or use remaining balance if not specified)
  4. Review the matched count displayed below the button
  5. Verify the matches in NetSuite before saving

Current Limitations

  • US Number Format Only: Amount parsing supports US number format only (comma for thousands, dot for decimal). Other formats may not parse correctly.
  • No Automatic Pagination: The extension works with the currently visible sublist page. If your invoices span multiple pages, you'll need to navigate pages manually.
  • Discount Field Detection: Discount support works by automatically detecting the "Disc. Taken" field. In rare cases with heavily customized NetSuite forms, manual verification may be needed.
  • Limited Testing: Tested in a limited number of NetSuite environments. Behavior may vary depending on your NetSuite configuration and customizations.
  • Not Full Automation: ApplyFast is a lightweight productivity tool designed to speed up manual cash application. It is not a full AR automation system.

These limitations are being addressed in future updates. Your feedback helps prioritize improvements!
